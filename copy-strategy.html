  2. She was curious enough to ask back. That is warm.

Positioning Angles (Ranked)

Angle 1 (PRIMARY): The Consultant Paradox

You teach your clients how to build systems on social media. You know what a leaky funnel looks like. You built the strategy for other brands that La Fosse Media has not yet deployed for itself.

Opener: "You asked back in March if this was something I do. It is. And I built you a plan specific to La Fosse Media to show you what it would look like."

Why it works: It is non-threatening because it mirrors her own expertise. She cannot dismiss it as irrelevant because she already knows the framework. The gap is something she has likely thought about herself. You are just naming it.

Angle 2 (SECONDARY): The Real Estate Vertical

Her published testimonials are three real estate professionals. She has a proven vertical and a repeatable case study but no system to capture that niche automatically.

Application: The lead magnet can be positioned as "Free Social Media Audit for Real Estate Professionals" - a niche-specific freebie that funnels her best ICP directly into her email list and positions her as the go-to social media consultant for realtors.

Angle 3 (SUPPORTING): The Email List Gap

6,337 Instagram followers. No email list visible. IG owns that audience, not her. One algorithm change, one shadow-ban, one policy update - and her prospecting surface is gone. An email list of 500 warm realtors is more valuable than 6,337 cold IG followers.

Application: Use this in the VSL and email sequence, not as the opening pitch. Opens too defensively if it is the first angle.
