The 5 Bottlenecks

Every gap is fixable.

you run your service business.

01
No Email Capture
High Revenue Impact

6,337 Instagram followers. No email opt-in, no freebie, no lead magnet anywhere on lafosse-media.com or in the bio link. The only capture mechanism is a waitlist form for new clients each quarter.

Instagram owns that audience, not you. One algorithm shift and your prospecting surface changes overnight. Every realtor who has ever liked a post is invisible to you after that.

Surgical Fix

A lead magnet for your real estate ICP: "Free Social Media Audit Template for Realtors" with an opt-in landing page and a five-email welcome sequence. Even a 3% conversion rate on 6,337 followers is 190 email subscribers you do not have today. At 10%, that is 634 warm leads you own. A list of 500 warm realtors at a 5% monthly conversion to a $97 audit offer is $2,400 per month passive.

02
No Productized Offer
High Revenue Impact

La Fosse Media sells hourly consulting, custom strategy buildouts, and custom retainers. All three require a discovery call, a custom scope, and a manual close. There is no product someone can buy without talking to you first.

Manual sales at any price point are a flat ceiling. There is no leverage in the current model.

Surgical Fix

A "Social Media Strategy Sprint" at $997 fixed scope, fixed price. One Loom audit recording of their account, a written strategy document with three content pillars and a 90-day content calendar, and one live 60-minute strategy call. Delivered within five business days. Someone reads the page, pays, and books their slot through Calendly. No discovery call, no custom scope. Four Sprints per month is $3,988 without adding a single retainer client.

03
No Public Pricing
Medium Revenue Impact

"Pricing discussed during discovery calls." No package tiers, no starting price, no range. Anyone who has been burned by an expensive agency bounces before filling the contact form. The only people who get through are referred or highly motivated.

Surgical Fix

At minimum, a visible starting price. Preferably a three-tier structure: the $997 Strategy Sprint, a social media audit at $297 to $497, and a monthly retainer range of $1,500 to $3,500. The current "select number of new clients each quarter" framing is strong. Pair it with a visible price range and the scarcity becomes aspirational rather than suspicious.

04
No Case Study or Portfolio Page
Medium Revenue Impact

Three testimonials on the homepage. No case study page, no portfolio, no before-and-after metrics, no results breakdown. The /work page on lafosse-media.com returns a 404. A potential client in real estate wants to know what happened to the last realtor who hired you.

Surgical Fix

One well-documented case study in your real estate vertical is worth ten generic testimonials. A 400-word narrative with two or three metrics becomes the proof section that converts the consideration-stage buyer who would otherwise bounce. The landing page this hub links to has a SWAP-SLOT for exactly this.

05
AEO 0/5 Citations
Medium Revenue Impact

Zero citations in AI overviews across five relevant search queries, including your strongest vertical ("social media strategy for realtors"). Your site has no FAQ schema, no Person schema, no Review schema visible in SERP. Zero citation presence across the AI-answer layer.

Surgical Fix

Person schema and FAQ schema installed on lafosse-media.com targeting the top four queries. One niche guide published: "Social Media Strategy for Real Estate Professionals: A 90-Day Playbook." Expected outcome: one to three AEO citations within 60 to 90 days. AG1 installs the schema and writes the guide in the first seven days of kickoff.
