Email 2 - Day 2

Subject: The audit mistake I see in almost every account

Hi [First Name],

Most small business accounts I audit have the same problem.

It is not the frequency. It is not even the content.

It is that there is no clear path from "I just saw your post" to "I am ready to hire you."

They post good content. People engage. Then those same people go to the bio, click the link, land on a website that says "Contact us for pricing," fill out a form, and wait.

That is three friction points before a single conversation starts.

The fix is not complicated. It is:

  1. A specific, clear CTA on every post
  2. A landing page that delivers one thing of value before asking for anything
  3. A follow-up sequence so the conversation continues even when you are not online

Most accounts are doing step one inconsistently and skipping two and three entirely.

The audit template I sent you on Day 1 will show you exactly where you stand. If you have not gone through it yet, set 20 minutes aside this week.

More on Thursday.

  • Courtney

Email 3 - Day 4

Subject: What happened when I audited a realtor's account

Hi [First Name],

I want to tell you about one of my real estate clients.

They came to me posting five times a week. Good photos, solid captions, decent engagement. But the phone was not ringing.

The audit showed three things:

First, their bio had no CTA and pointed to a generic homepage instead of their listings page.

Second, they were posting market updates and home photos - useful, but zero content that answered the question "why should I work with you specifically?"

Third, they had no follow-up system. Someone could like 20 posts, send one DM, and if the reply did not land within 24 hours, the conversation was gone.

We fixed the bio in one afternoon. Added a lead magnet for buyers looking in their market. Set up a simple 5-email follow-up sequence.

The social media did not change. The system around it did.

They started booking consistent consultation calls within 60 days.

That is what a strategy buildout actually looks like in practice.

If you want to see what it would look like for your business, I have a few new client spots opening in the next quarter. Details in my next email.
