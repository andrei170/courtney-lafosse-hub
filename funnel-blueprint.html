Funnel Blueprint - La Fosse Media Corp

Built: 2026-05-17 Prospect: Courtney La Fossé Objective: Convert existing IG audience into owned email list, productized offer buyers, and consult call bookings for her retainer

Problems: Zero owned audience. 100% manual sales. Every client requires time before any money changes hands. No passive income layer. No follow-up system for people who visit the site but do not book.

Proposed Funnel

Stage 0: Content (Already Exists - Optimize for CTA)

Platform: Instagram (primary), LinkedIn (secondary) Current: 503 posts, no consistent conversion CTA Fix: Every post ends with one of three CTAs: - "Free audit template - link in bio" - "DM me AUDIT for the free checklist" - "Book a free 20-min intro call - calendly in bio"

No post leaves without a direction.

Stage 1: Lead Magnet

Name: "Free Social Media Audit Template: 12 Questions Every Small Business Should Ask Before Posting Another Piece of Content"

Format: PDF (2 pages) + optional Loom walkthrough video (5 min)

Delivery: Email opt-in page hosted at lafosse-media.com/free-audit or a standalone landing page (Carrd, ConvertKit landing, or HoneyBook page - whatever she already uses)

ICP targeting: Broad enough for all small businesses, with a "For Real Estate Professionals" variant as a secondary version for her proven vertical.

Expected conversion rate: 5-15% of people who click through from IG bio. At 6,337 followers and a 2% weekly active traffic assumption, that is 127 people/week seeing the bio link. At 10% opt-in, ~13 new subscribers per week or 50-60/month.

This is the load-bearing piece of the funnel. Nothing else matters until this exists.

Stage 2: Welcome Email Sequence (5 Emails, 10 Days)

See email-sequence.md for full copy.

Email 1 (Day 0): Deliver the audit template + who Courtney is Email 2 (Day 2): The single biggest mistake she sees in social media audits Email 3 (Day 4): The real estate social media case study (proof) Email 4 (Day 7): Introduce the productized offer (Social Media Strategy Sprint) Email 5 (Day 10): The invitation - consult call or waitlist

Goal of sequence: Move the subscriber from "free template" to "booked intro call" or "purchased strategy sprint" within 10 days of opting in.

Stage 3: Productized Mid-Tier Offer

Name: "Social Media Strategy Sprint"

Price: $997 (recommended) or $797 (early adopter)

What it includes: - Full social media audit of their account (30 min Loom recording) - Written strategy document: 3 content pillars, 90-day content calendar, bio optimization, lead magnet recommendation - One live 60-minute strategy call to walk through and adjust - Delivered within 5 business days

This is fixed scope, fixed price, no custom negotiation. Someone reads the sales page, pays, books their audit call slot through Calendly, receives the Loom + document within 5 days. No back-and-forth required from Courtney until the strategy call.

Revenue math: 4 Strategy Sprints per month = $3,988/month. 8 per month = $7,976/month. Neither number requires adding a single new client to her existing retainer load.

Stage 4: Consult Call to Retainer (Existing Path, Optimized)

What changes: - Add a self-book link (Calendly or similar) visible in the IG bio, on the contact page, and at the bottom of every email sequence - Add a "what to expect" pre-call page (30-second explainer: "In our 20 minutes, we will look at your current social media, identify the 2-3 highest-leverage moves, and see if a retainer or strategy buildout is the right fit") - Add pricing anchoring on the website: "Strategy buildouts from $XXX. Monthly retainers from $XXX/month."

Retainer funnel route: Email sequence Email 5 leads to a "book a call" CTA. Strategy Sprint purchasers who want ongoing support get offered a retainer at a discounted entry rate ("Strategy Sprint clients skip the waitlist").

Stage 5: AEO Layer (90-Day Play)

Schema markup: Add Person schema, FAQ schema, and LocalBusiness schema to lafosse-media.com. Target queries: "social media consultant for realtors," "social media strategy for small businesses [Orange County]," "Instagram strategy consultant."

Niche guide: Publish one 1,500-word guide titled "Social Media Strategy for Real Estate Professionals: A 90-Day Playbook" - targets the exact ICP of her three published testimonials.

Expected outcome: 1-3 AEO citations in AI overviews within 60-90 days. Even one citation in "best social media consultant for realtors" surfaces her to a buyer-intent audience she currently does not reach.
