Bottleneck Analysis - La Fosse Media Corp

Built: 2026-05-17 Prospect: Courtney La Fossé, Social Media Marketing Consultant Basis: Full site audit (lafosse-media.com), IG SERP, LinkedIn SERP, AEO gap check

Summary

La Fosse Media has a working service business, a clean website, and a genuine 10-year track record. The bottlenecks are not about quality - they are about infrastructure. The funnel stops at "DM me" and the contact form. Everything after that is manual.

Bottleneck 1: No Email Capture - Platform Risk Is Total

What is happening: 6,337 Instagram followers. No email opt-in, no freebie, no lead magnet anywhere on the site or in the bio link. The only capture mechanism is a waitlist form for new clients.

Why it matters: Instagram owns that audience, not Courtney. The algorithm decides who sees her content. If IG changes its rules, shadowbans her account, or she takes a week off, the top of her funnel goes dark. Every realtor, coach, and healthcare operator who ever liked a post is lost.

The fix: A simple lead magnet - "Free Social Media Audit Template for Small Business Owners" or "Free Social Media Checklist for Realtors" - with a landing page and an email capture form. Even a 3% conversion rate on 6,337 followers is 190 email subscribers she does not have today. At 10%, that is 634 warm leads she owns.

Revenue implication: A list of 500 warm realtors at a 5% monthly conversion to a $97 audit offer is $2,400/month passive. That does not include the consult calls it feeds.

Bottleneck 2: No Productized Offer - Every Sale Is Manual

What is happening: La Fosse Media sells hourly consulting, custom strategy buildouts, and custom retainers. All three require a discovery call, a custom scope, and a manual close. There is no product someone can buy without talking to her first.

Why it matters: Manual sales at a low price point are unsustainable. If she is doing $5K-$15K/month in services, she is trading time for money at a flat rate. There is no leverage in the model.

The fix: A productized offer between "free content" and "custom retainer." Options: - A $197-$497 Social Media Audit Report (delivered async via Loom + PDF) - A $997 "Social Media Strategy Sprint" (1 call + full strategy document, fixed scope, fixed price) - A $297 "Realtor Social Media Playbook" (PDF + video walkthrough, niche-specific)

A fixed-scope, fixed-price product converts from email marketing, does not require a call, and builds the pipeline for the higher-ticket retainer.

Bottleneck 3: No Public Pricing - Friction Before the Conversation Starts

What is happening: "Pricing discussed during discovery calls." No package tiers, no starting price, no range, nothing public.

Why it matters: Anyone landing on the site who has been burned by an expensive agency goes quiet when they see "contact us for pricing." It reads as "probably expensive." They bounce before filling the form. The only people who get through are either referred or highly motivated.

The fix: At minimum, a starting price or a "from $XXX" signal. Preferably a simple 3-tier structure with approximate ranges for the strategy buildout, the audit, and the monthly retainer. Transparency converts better than mystery in the B2B service space.

Copywriting note: The site currently says "takes on a select number of new clients each quarter." That framing implies scarcity and quality, which is good. Pair it with a visible price range and the scarcity becomes aspirational rather than suspicious.

Bottleneck 4: No Case Study or Portfolio Page

What is happening: Three testimonials on the homepage. No case study page, no portfolio, no before/after metrics, no results breakdown. The /work page returns a 404.

Why it matters: Social media consultants live and die by proof. A potential client in real estate wants to know what happened to the last realtor who hired her. "Conrad P., Realtor" saying something positive is not the same as "Conrad came in at 300 followers, we built a content strategy, and in 4 months his listings page was generating 3-4 inbound calls per week."

The fix: A single well-documented case study in her real estate vertical is worth 10 generic testimonials. It does not need to be a 2,000-word writeup - a 400-word narrative with 2-3 metrics is enough. This becomes the proof section on the hub page.

Bottleneck 5: AEO Gap - 0 of 5 Queries, Zero AI Presence

What is happening: Courtney has zero citations in AI overviews across five relevant search queries, including her strongest vertical ("social media strategy for realtors").

Why it matters: When a realtor in Orange County asks ChatGPT or Google's AI Overview "who should I hire for my social media strategy," Courtney does not exist in the answer. Her competitors who appear in those answers get the warm-intent buyer who is ready to pay. She gets none of that traffic.

The fix: AEO is not about content volume - it is about structure. Three things drive it: 1. A clear "Who I Help" page with FAQ schema markup (structured data telling AI crawlers what questions she answers) 2. Publishing a niche guide with real specificity ("Social Media Strategy for Real Estate: A 90-Day Playbook") 3. Getting cited in 2-3 industry roundups or local business press

This is a 60-90 day play. AG1 can install the schema and write the niche guide in the first 7 days.

Bottleneck Severity Summary

All five are fixable within 30 days. None require a rebuild of her existing service business.
