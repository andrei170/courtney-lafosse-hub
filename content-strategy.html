Phase 3

Content Strategy - La Fosse Media Corp

Built: 2026-05-17 Prospect: Courtney La Fossé, Social Media Marketing Consultant Platform: Instagram (primary), LinkedIn (secondary)

Voice + Tone Direction

Courtney's current positioning ("social media should work for you, not the other way around") is strategist-first - measured, intentional, sustainability-oriented. Her audience is small business owners who are exhausted by social media, not aspiring influencers.

The content strategy below amplifies that positioning while solving her three biggest content gaps: - She posts tips but has no conversion architecture - She shows expertise but has no proof of outcomes - She attracts followers but does not capture them into an owned list

Every pillar below has a call-to-action layer that feeds the lead magnet funnel.

Content Pillars

Pillar 1: Strategy Clarity (40% of content)

Posts that simplify strategy concepts her audience finds confusing or overwhelming.

Format: Carousel (highest saves), Reel (fastest reach), occasional static graphic Topics: Content calendar basics, pillar structure, why most IG accounts plateau, how to write a bio that converts, the difference between a strategy and a content plan CTA pattern: "Want my free audit template? Link in bio."

Pillar 2: Proof Moments (25% of content)

Client wins, behind-the-scenes of her work, and process snapshots.

Format: Static with quote overlay, short Reel, text-only IG story slide Topics: "Here is what a real estate social media strategy looks like in month 3," before/after content performance, client review screenshots, "I just finished a strategy buildout for [industry] - here is the thing that surprised me" CTA pattern: Tag, share, or "DM me 'AUDIT' to get my free template"

Pillar 3: The Consultant Lens (20% of content)

Posts that address her audience as a peer - other social media consultants, virtual assistants, and freelancers who manage clients' social media.

Format: Talking-head Reel, carousel checklist Topics: How to price a social media retainer, what to include in a strategy document, handling clients who want to do it themselves, how to raise your rates CTA pattern: "I built a full template - grab it free at the link in bio."

Pillar 4: Personal Authority (15% of content)

Courtney's perspective, her 10-year read on what works, her opinions.

Format: Talking-head Reel, text-heavy static, IG Stories Topics: What social media was like before algorithm changes, a mistake she made in year 3, the one thing all her best clients have in common, why she only takes on a select number of clients per quarter CTA pattern: None - this is relationship/trust building. Let it breathe.

Lead Magnet Hook Posts (Use Anytime)

These posts exist solely to drive opt-ins for the free lead magnet:

  1. "I have the exact social media audit checklist I use with every new client. I am making it free this week. Comment AUDIT and I will send it to you." (Comment DM automation - high engagement hack)

  2. Carousel: "12 questions to ask before you hire a social media consultant" - last slide: "I turned this into a free audit template. Get it at the link in bio."

  3. Reel: "If I could only give you one piece of social media advice - it would be this [hook]. But if you want the full checklist, it is linked in my bio."

LinkedIn Content (Secondary, 2x/month)

LinkedIn posts work for Courtney because her ICP (realtors, coaches, healthcare operators) uses LinkedIn professionally. Two posts per month is enough to maintain presence without overextending.

Format: Long-form text post (500-800 words), no image required Topics: 1. "Why small businesses are getting social media wrong in 2026 (and what works instead)" 2. "I have done 50+ social media audits. Here are the 5 things I always find."

CTA: Link to the lead magnet or the lafosse-media.com contact page.

Hashtag Strategy (IG)

Primary (high relevance, moderate competition): #socialmediatips, #socialmediamarketing, #socialmediastrategist, #instagramstrategy, #contentmarketing

Niche (lower competition, higher conversion): #realtorsocialmedia, #socialmediaforcoaches, #socialmediaforsmallbusiness, #contentcalendar, #instagramforbusiness

Local (reach + credibility): #orangecountybusiness, #socalentrepreneur, #socalmedia (pun intended, low competition)

Rule: 8-12 hashtags per post. Mix one primary + two niche + one local per post minimum.

Stories Strategy

Stories are the conversion layer - they warm followers who already watch content into action-takers.

Weekly Story sequence: - Day 1 (Mon): Share the feed post + one line of added context - Day 3 (Wed): Poll or question sticker ("What is the hardest part of social media for your business?") - Day 5 (Fri): Lead magnet CTA ("I built a free checklist. It is in my link. Grab it this weekend.") - Weekend: Behind-the-scenes of her week, client call context without names, personal moment

Stories should NOT be sales-heavy every day. The ratio is 3:1 - three posts of genuine value or connection, one with a CTA.
