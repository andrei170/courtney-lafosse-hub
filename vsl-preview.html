Pre-Record Notes

Record this as a screen share with face cam in the corner. Open the hub page (https://andrei170.github.io/courtney-lafosse-hub/) on screen. Walk through it live. This is NOT a polished production - it is a Loom-style walkthrough that feels like a colleague showing their work.

Total time: 2:45 to 3:15. Do not go over 3:30.

Script

[0:00 - 0:20] - The Callback

"Hey Courtney. Andrei here. Back in March you replied to a DM I sent you asking if what I do is a service I provide. It is. And I have been wanting to send you this follow-up for a while. I finally built it out properly, so here it is."

[Pause. Let that land.]

"This is a three-minute walkthrough of what I put together specifically for La Fosse Media."

[0:20 - 0:55] - The Observation (The Bombshell)

[Screen switches to lafosse-media.com - show the homepage]

"I spent time going through your site and your Instagram. You have a clean setup, solid services, good positioning. Ten years in the niche, and the work is clearly real - the real estate testimonials on the homepage tell that story.

But here is the thing I kept coming back to: you teach your clients how to build systems on social media. The content pillar structure, the lead capture, the follow-up sequence. And La Fosse Media itself is not running those systems yet.

Not as a criticism - this is the most common pattern I see with consultants and service providers. The cobbler's kids have no shoes. You are so busy delivering the strategy for other businesses that the architecture for your own business is not there yet."

[0:55 - 1:40] - The Three Gaps

[Still on lafosse-media.com, scroll slowly]

"Three specific things:

One - no email capture. Your site has a waitlist form and a contact form. Neither of them builds an owned list. You have 6,300 followers on Instagram and Instagram owns all of them. One algorithm shift and your prospecting surface changes overnight. A simple lead magnet - a free audit template, a checklist for realtors - and a landing page converts that following into a list you own.

Two - no productized offer. Everything you sell requires a discovery call and a custom close. A fixed-scope audit package at a visible price point is something someone can buy on a Tuesday at 10pm without getting on a call with you. That is not replacing your retainer work - it is adding a conversion layer before the call.

Three - zero AEO citations. I checked five queries. You do not show up in any of them. Not in 'social media consultant for realtors,' not in 'best Instagram strategy consultant.' The AI overview layer captures warm-intent buyers you are not seeing."

[1:40 - 2:30] - The Plan

[Switch to the hub page - https://andrei170.github.io/courtney-lafosse-hub/]

"So I built this. It is a full plan for La Fosse Media - the funnel architecture, the content calendar, the email sequence, the productized offer structure. Everything is specific to you, not a template."

[Scroll through the hub sections]

"The lead magnet I would build first is a free Social Media Audit Template - branded to La Fosse Media, specifically for small business owners in real estate, coaching, and healthcare. Your exact ICP. An opt-in landing page, a five-email welcome sequence, and a mid-tier Strategy Sprint offer at $997 that converts from the email list without a sales call.

All of this installs in under seven days from kickoff. None of it requires you to change how you run your retainers or your one-time buildout work. It is a layer on top of what already exists."

[2:30 - 3:00] - The Close

[Face cam only, no screen share]

"I want to be straight with you. I am not sending this to a hundred consultants. I picked La Fosse Media because the gap is specific and the fix is direct. You already know this stuff. I am just building it for you instead of for a client.

Our best result - client went from zero to 172K a month in the first month we worked together. I am not promising that for every business. But I can tell you the funnel infrastructure I am proposing here is the same architecture that got them there.

If you want to talk through it, the hub has a call link at the bottom. Or just reply to the DM. Either works."

[Short pause]

"The plan is built. Up to you whether it goes anywhere."
